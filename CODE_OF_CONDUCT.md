# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Energy Icons a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include being respectful, accepting criticism, and focusing on what is best for the library.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, trolling, insulting comments, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through a GitHub issue or by contacting the maintainers of the [Sam-r-passmore](https://github.com/Sam-r-passmore) organisation. Reports are reviewed and result in whatever action is considered appropriate, including a temporary or permanent ban.
